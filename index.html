02 / From find to decision

How to use an
ACBuy spreadsheet.

An ACBuy spreadsheet brings product links into one place. Its value comes from helping you compare candidates, record details and identify what still needs checking.

A catalog entry alone does not establish current purchasing support. Confirm the exact source link, product category and destination restrictions with your selected service before placing an order.

Choose a precise starting point

Search for a product type and feature: canvas low-tops, a fleece hoodie or a graphic tee. Keep a shortlist of three items.

Match the original record

Save the catalog ID, source link, variant, currency and price date. An item number here identifies a HipobuyFind entry, not a marketplace seller ID.

Resolve fit and evidence

Compare measurements with something you own. Ask for relevant inspection photos of the actual order where the service offers them.

Budget the complete parcel

Check delivery charges, service fees, exchange costs, international shipping and taxes before deciding whether to proceed.

03 / Know what to check

A good photo is the beginning.

Fit & product checks

  • Shoes: compare insole measurements, width and the selected size system. EU, US and UK labels need careful matching.
  • Hoodies and T-shirts: measure chest width, body length and shoulders in centimetres. “Oversized” is a fit description, not a measurement.
  • Bags: record height, width, depth and strap length. Check closures, lining and interior capacity.
  • Every category: confirm material claims, colour, included pieces and the exact price-changing option.

QC & order checks

  • Separate listing photos from QC: catalog pictures show an advertised item; warehouse inspection should document your order.
  • Ask a specific question: request a measurement, close-up of a seam or a clear view of a fastening instead of “Is it good?”
  • Check return terms early: note the return deadline, seller exclusions and who pays domestic return delivery.
  • Keep an order record: save the original link, variant, payment reference and written answers for later comparison.
04 / The actual budget

The item price
is one part.

Use a single currency throughout this planner. Enter your own quotes; the calculator does not fetch exchange rates, fees or carrier prices.

For a multi-item haul, add the item amounts first. Allocate shared shipping only after you know the parcel estimate. Ask the carrier or agent whether actual weight or dimensional weight determines the charge.

Planning formula: items + domestic delivery + service / exchange charges + international shipping + estimated tax / duty. This is an estimate, not a checkout quote.

05 / Useful answers

Before you open another tab.

What is an ACBuy spreadsheet?

An ACBuy spreadsheet is an index used to discover product listings and organize shopping research. It may be a traditional sheet or a searchable website. Each item still needs its own price, sizing, seller and purchasing-service checks.

Where do the two main buttons go?

“ACBuy spreadsheet” and “Buy it now” both open https://hipobuyfind.com. They open an external product-discovery catalog, not a checkout or an official ACBuy service.

Are these prices live and inclusive of shipping?

No. The displayed prices are a catalog snapshot reviewed on November 19, 2026. USD amounts are the source catalog’s approximate equivalents. Shipping, service fees, exchange costs and applicable taxes must be checked separately.

Do product images count as QC photos?

No. These are catalog listing images. QC means inspection evidence for the actual ordered item, including relevant measurements, condition and variant details.

Can every catalog item be purchased through ACBuy?

A catalog listing does not establish purchasing-service eligibility. Confirm the exact source link, product category, current service restrictions and shipping route directly with the provider before paying.

How should I handle a removed or changed listing?

Use the saved item ID to check your records, then reopen the source page. If the variant, seller or price has changed, treat it as a new decision. Do not assume that a similar thumbnail represents the same item.

Is this an official ACBuy website?

No. This page is an independent informational resource. It does not process payments, inspect goods, ship parcels or provide official agent support. Brand and service names identify the subjects discussed and do not imply affiliation.
